Privacy at Inactive Studios

How Inactive Studios LLC handles information across this website and our games.

Our approach

Inactive Studios LLC designs its website and games around data minimization. We do not sell personal information. We do not currently require an account to use this website, and this website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers.

Website information

This website is informational and is hosted through third-party infrastructure. Those providers may process routine technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata, and security logs as necessary to deliver and protect their services.

Communications

If you email us, we receive the address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your inquiry or provide support and retain it only as reasonably necessary for those purposes and applicable business or legal requirements.
