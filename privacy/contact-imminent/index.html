Contact Imminent Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Inactive Studios LLC handles information in connection with Contact Imminent.

About this policy

Contact Imminent is developed and published by Inactive Studios LLC, Utah, United States. This policy describes the data practices intended for the Google Play release of Contact Imminent and will be maintained to reflect the services and software included in the released application.

Inactive Studios follows a data-minimization approach. We do not sell player personal information and do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary to operate, support, secure, diagnose, or monetize the game.

Game data and local storage

Contact Imminent may store game settings, preferences, progress, and other gameplay state locally on the user's device. Local game data is used to provide gameplay functionality and is not, by itself, a player account or cloud profile maintained by Inactive Studios.

Removing the application or clearing its local storage may remove locally stored game data, subject to the behavior of the device operating system and platform backup features.

Player accounts and cloud saves

Contact Imminent does not currently require or provide an Inactive Studios player account, and Inactive Studios does not currently operate a Contact Imminent cloud-save service. Accordingly, there is no Contact Imminent player-account database or studio-hosted cloud-save profile to delete.

If account or cloud functionality is introduced in a future release, this policy and applicable storefront disclosures will be updated before or when that functionality is offered.

Advertising

The planned Google Play release of Contact Imminent uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising. Depending on device settings, consent choices, region, and advertising configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, general device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes including delivering, measuring, limiting, securing, and, where permitted, personalizing advertisements.

Where required, Contact Imminent will use Google's User Messaging Platform or related Google privacy controls to present consent or privacy choices before advertising data is processed in a manner requiring consent. Available advertising choices may vary by region and applicable law.

Inactive Studios does not sell player personal information. Advertising providers process information according to their own applicable privacy terms and the configuration selected by Inactive Studios.

Crash reporting and diagnostics

The planned Google Play release of Contact Imminent uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to help identify crashes, application-not-responding events, and software stability problems.

Crashlytics and supporting Firebase services may process diagnostic and technical information such as crash stack traces, application state associated with a crash, app and app-version information, device model and operating-system information, session information, and installation identifiers. This information is used to diagnose defects, improve stability, and maintain the game.

Contact Imminent does not currently use Firebase Analytics as a separate player-behavior analytics service. If analytics functionality is introduced, this policy and applicable storefront disclosures will be reviewed and updated.

Purchases and Google Play services

Contact Imminent may offer digital products or other in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment credentials and payment processing are handled by Google Play rather than directly by Inactive Studios. Inactive Studios may receive transaction-related information necessary to provide purchased content, verify transactions, maintain business records, provide support, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

Google Play and other platform services may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Information you provide to us

If you contact Inactive Studios for support, privacy questions, or other communications, we receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your email address, message content, attachments, and technical details you include in your request. We use this information to respond to the request, troubleshoot issues, protect our services, or meet applicable business and legal obligations.

Permissions and sensitive information

Contact Imminent is not designed to require access to a user's contacts, precise location, health information, SMS messages, call logs, microphone, or camera for its core gameplay. Inactive Studios seeks to avoid requesting sensitive Android permissions that are unnecessary to the game's functionality.

If a future feature requires access to additional personal or sensitive information, the feature, permission request, applicable in-app disclosure or consent flow, this policy, and storefront disclosures will be reviewed before release.

How information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate and support Contact Imminent, including Google services associated with advertising, crash diagnostics, application distribution, and payment processing. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, protect users or services, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and safety of Inactive Studios or others.

Inactive Studios does not sell personal and sensitive user data.

Security

Inactive Studios uses reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information it handles. Network communications under our control that contain personal or sensitive information are intended to use encrypted transport such as HTTPS. Third-party services used by Contact Imminent maintain their own security practices and infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Inactive Studios retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including support, diagnostics, security, transaction records, business operations, and applicable legal obligations.

Local gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application, subject to operating-system or platform backup behavior.

Contact Imminent does not currently provide an Inactive Studios player account or studio-hosted cloud-save profile. If account creation is introduced, Inactive Studios will provide applicable account-deletion mechanisms and update this policy accordingly.

Privacy or deletion requests concerning information held directly by Inactive Studios may be submitted to privacy@inactivestudios.com. Requests concerning information controlled independently by third-party providers may also be subject to those providers' privacy controls and retention requirements.

Children's privacy

Contact Imminent is not designed or directed specifically to children. The intended audience and advertising configuration for each storefront release will be established through the applicable platform's age-rating and audience processes. Inactive Studios does not knowingly use the game to solicit personal information from children.

International users

Contact Imminent may be available in multiple jurisdictions. Data may be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence by Inactive Studios or its service providers. Where applicable, we and our service providers are responsible for handling information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Third-party services

The Google Play release may use services provided by Google, including Google Play, Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google's User Messaging Platform, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Installations, and Firebase Sessions. The exact services present in a released build are reviewed as part of Inactive Studios' release process, and this policy and storefront disclosures are maintained to reflect the released configuration.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Contact Imminent, its third-party services, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL and identified by the effective and last-updated dates below.
