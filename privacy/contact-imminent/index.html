Contact Imminent Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Inactive Studios LLC handles information in connection with Contact Imminent.

About this policy

Contact Imminent is developed and published by Inactive Studios LLC, Utah, United States. This policy describes the data practices of the Google Play release of Contact Imminent, including information handled by Inactive Studios and information processed through third-party services integrated into the application.

Inactive Studios follows a data-minimization approach. We do not sell player personal information and do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary to operate, support, secure, diagnose, or monetize the game.

Game data and local storage

Contact Imminent may store game settings, preferences, progress, and other gameplay state locally on the user's device. Local game data is used to provide gameplay functionality and is not, by itself, a player account or cloud profile maintained by Inactive Studios.

Removing the application or clearing its local storage may remove locally stored game data, subject to the behavior of the device operating system and platform backup features.

Player accounts and cloud saves

Contact Imminent does not require or provide an Inactive Studios player account, and Inactive Studios does not operate a Contact Imminent cloud-save service. Accordingly, there is no Contact Imminent player-account database or studio-hosted cloud-save profile to delete.

If account or cloud functionality is introduced in a future release, this policy and applicable storefront disclosures will be reviewed and updated to reflect that functionality.

Advertising

Contact Imminent uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising.

According to Google's documentation for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. This information includes the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; user-product interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views; diagnostic and performance information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The availability, collection, or transmission of some identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, may depend on device settings, application configuration, privacy choices, applicable requirements, and advertising configuration.

Inactive Studios uses advertising services to monetize Contact Imminent. Information processed through the advertising SDK may also be used by Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes in accordance with Google's applicable terms, privacy policies, service configuration, and the user's available privacy choices.

Inactive Studios does not sell player personal information.

Advertising consent and privacy choices

Contact Imminent uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

The application requests updated consent information through UMP and, when required for a user or region, presents the applicable privacy or consent message before requesting advertising in accordance with the resulting consent status and advertising configuration.

Where required, Contact Imminent provides access to applicable privacy options so users can review or change available privacy choices, including withdrawing consent where required by applicable law.

The specific choices and messages presented may vary according to the user's region, applicable legal requirements, Google's configured privacy messages, device settings, and advertising configuration.

Crash reporting and diagnostics

Contact Imminent uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to identify crashes, application-not-responding events, and software-stability problems.

According to Google's Firebase documentation, Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash stack traces, relevant application state, relevant device metadata, and a Crashlytics installation UUID. Information associated with crash reporting may include crash timestamps, application identifier and version information, operating-system information, device model and hardware characteristics, and identifiers used to associate crash and session information.

Firebase Crashlytics has dependencies on Firebase Installations and Firebase Sessions. Firebase Installations generates and collects a Firebase Installation ID associated with an installation of the application and Firebase user-agent information. Firebase Sessions collects application metadata, device metadata, and application/session metrics used to provide crash and application-quality metrics.

Contact Imminent uses this information to identify defects, investigate crashes and application stability, and improve and maintain the game.

Inactive Studios does not use an Inactive Studios player-account identifier with Crashlytics because Contact Imminent does not provide an Inactive Studios player account. The production configuration is not intended to attach names, email addresses, or other Inactive Studios account information to crash reports.

Contact Imminent does not use Firebase Analytics as a separate player-behavior analytics service. If separate analytics functionality is introduced in a future release, this policy and applicable storefront disclosures will be reviewed and updated.

Purchases and Google Play services

Contact Imminent may offer digital products or other in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment credentials and payment processing are handled by Google Play rather than directly by Inactive Studios. Inactive Studios may receive transaction-related information necessary to provide purchased content, verify transactions, maintain business records, provide support, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

Google Play and other platform services may independently process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

Information you provide to us

If you contact Inactive Studios for support, privacy questions, or other communications, we receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your email address, message content, attachments, and technical details you include in your request. We use this information to respond to the request, troubleshoot issues, protect our services, or meet applicable business and legal obligations.

Permissions and sensitive information

Contact Imminent is not designed to require access to a user's contacts, precise location, health information, SMS messages, call logs, microphone, or camera for its core gameplay. Inactive Studios seeks to avoid requesting sensitive Android permissions that are unnecessary to the game's functionality.

If a future feature requires access to additional personal or sensitive information, the feature, permission request, applicable in-app disclosure or consent flow, this policy, and applicable storefront disclosures will be reviewed before release.

How information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate and support Contact Imminent. These include Google services associated with advertising and advertising consent, crash diagnostics and application-quality monitoring, application distribution, and payment processing. The information processed and the purposes of processing vary by service as described elsewhere in this policy and in the applicable provider documentation and privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or legal process; protect users, services, rights, property, or safety; or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Inactive Studios does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Security

Inactive Studios uses reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information it handles. Network communications under our control that contain personal or sensitive information are intended to use encrypted transport such as HTTPS. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS, and Firebase services use encrypted transport for data in transit as described in Google's applicable documentation. Third-party services used by Contact Imminent maintain their own security practices and infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Inactive Studios retains information under its control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including support, diagnostics, security, transaction records, business operations, dispute resolution, and applicable legal obligations. Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' applicable service-specific retention practices and legal obligations.

Local gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application, subject to operating-system or platform backup behavior.

Contact Imminent does not provide an Inactive Studios player account or studio-hosted cloud-save profile. If account creation is introduced in a future release, Inactive Studios will implement applicable account-deletion mechanisms and update this policy and applicable storefront disclosures accordingly.

Privacy or deletion requests concerning information held directly by Inactive Studios may be submitted to privacy@inactivestudios.com. Requests concerning information controlled independently by third-party providers may also be subject to those providers' privacy controls and retention requirements.

Children's privacy

Contact Imminent is not designed or directed specifically to children. The intended audience and advertising configuration for each storefront release are established through the applicable platform's age-rating and audience processes. Inactive Studios does not knowingly use the game to solicit personal information from children.

International users

Contact Imminent may be available in multiple jurisdictions. Information may be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence by Inactive Studios or its service providers. Where applicable, Inactive Studios and its service providers are responsible for handling information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Third-party services

The Google Play release of Contact Imminent uses Google services including Google Play, Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google's User Messaging Platform, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Installations, and Firebase Sessions.

These services may process information on behalf of Inactive Studios or independently under Google's applicable terms, privacy policies, service configurations, and legal obligations. Their data practices may differ according to the service, application configuration, device settings, privacy choices, region, and applicable law.

Inactive Studios reviews the third-party SDKs and services included in each production release and maintains this Privacy Policy and applicable storefront disclosures to reflect the released configuration.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Contact Imminent, its third-party services, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL and identified by the effective and last-updated dates below.
